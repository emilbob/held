=== HOW IT WAS MADE (thread, reply under the video) ===

1/
How we made this 55-second Halloween film for Held: no camera, no stock footage, no paid assets. It's all code, and it cost $0.

🧵

2/
Everything was built in Blender 5.2 with Python scripts: the firelit room, the carved jack-o'-lantern, the ghost, the laptop, the phone and the HELD package.

The pumpkin is a real 3D model, hollowed out and carved, and a candle inside lights its face.

3/
The screens use the real Held app: its own stylesheet and wording, captured in headless Chrome.

The checkout, "Payment held", the refused payout on the ghost's phone and "Paid to merchant" are the app's real states.

4/
The armchair, candlesticks, clock and the wood and brick textures are free CC0 assets from Poly Haven.

The fire, the candle flames and the smoke are made in Blender's shaders, without any simulation.

5/
The music and the sound effects are original too, synthesized in Python: piano, celesta, strings, fire crackle, wind and every thud.

No samples, no music library.

6/
Rendered with Eevee on a MacBook Air M4: 1320 frames at 1280×720, in about 10 hours.

7/
Built with @claudeai (Claude Code): Claude wrote the 3D scenes, the animation, the soundtrack and the edit. We directed every shot.
